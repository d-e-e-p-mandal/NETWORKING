# FINAL OBJECTIVE

After completing this syllabus, you should be able to:

1.  Explain computer networking from physical transmission to
    application protocols.
2.  Understand and troubleshoot OSI and TCP/IP layers.
3.  Design IPv4 and IPv6 addressing plans and perform subnetting, CIDR,
    VLSM, and route summarization.
4.  Configure and troubleshoot Ethernet, switching, VLANs, trunking,
    STP, RSTP, MSTP, and EtherChannel.
5.  Configure and understand static routing and major dynamic routing
    protocols including RIP, EIGRP, OSPF, IS-IS, and BGP.
6.  Understand and troubleshoot DHCP, DNS, NAT, ICMP, TCP, UDP, and
    common network services.
7.  Design and secure enterprise networks using ACLs, firewalls,
    IDS/IPS, AAA, NAC, 802.1X, VPN, IPsec, TLS, PKI, certificates, and
    mTLS.
8.  Understand load balancing, reverse proxies, forward proxies, high
    availability, and application-aware traffic routing.
9.  Understand enterprise WAN, MPLS, SDN, SD-WAN, data center fabrics,
    VXLAN, EVPN, VRF, and ECMP.
10. Design cloud networks using AWS, Azure, and GCP networking services.
11. Automate network operations using Python, REST APIs, Netmiko,
    Ansible, Terraform, NETCONF, RESTCONF, YANG, and OpenConfig.
12. Monitor networks using SNMP, Syslog, NTP, metrics, dashboards, and
    packet-analysis tools.
13. Troubleshoot real-world failures involving DNS, DHCP, VLANs,
    routing, STP, BGP, NAT, ACLs, MTU, TCP, TLS, VPNs, and load
    balancers.
14. Configure Cisco IOS networking services and build practical Packet
    Tracer, GNS3, and EVE-NG labs.
15. Design, document, secure, automate, monitor, troubleshoot, and
    maintain production-oriented enterprise networks.

**Target level:** Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Professional →
Network Architect.
